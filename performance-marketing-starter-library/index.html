How to use this

Two days, about 3–4 hours each. Go in order — fundamentals first, then the platforms, then measurement. You won't master any of it in 48 hours, and that's fine. The goal is the language and the mental model, so nothing in the sessions sounds foreign.

Key terms to know

Learn these cold. They're the shorthand every session will assume you already speak.

ImpressionOne time your ad was shown.
ReachThe number of unique people who saw it.
FrequencyAverage times each person saw your ad.
CTRClick-through rate — clicks ÷ impressions.
CPCCost per click.
CPMCost per 1,000 impressions.
CVRConversion rate — conversions ÷ clicks.
CPA / CPLCost per acquisition, or per lead.
CACCost to acquire one paying customer.
ROASRevenue ÷ ad spend. The core efficiency number.
AOVAverage order value.
LTVLifetime value of a customer.
Pixel / CAPICode that sends conversion data back to the platform.
Conversion eventThe action you count as success (purchase, lead).
Advantage+Meta's AI-automated campaign type.
Performance MaxGoogle's AI-automated, all-inventory campaign.
LookalikeA new audience modelled on your best customers.
RetargetingAds shown to people who already engaged.
Quality ScoreGoogle's rating of your keyword/ad relevance.
AttributionHow credit for a conversion is assigned across touches.
