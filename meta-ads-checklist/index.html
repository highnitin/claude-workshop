Conversions API (CAPI)

Why it matters: since iOS 14, browser-only tracking leaks badly. CAPI sends conversions server-side and typically recovers 20–40% of the signal you'd otherwise lose — treat it as essential, not optional.

07

Account structure blueprint

A clean starting architecture for a new account:

Top of Funnel (TOF)
└─ Campaign: [Brand]_TOF_Awareness
   ├─ Ad Set: Broad / Interest-based
   └─ Ad Set: Lookalike audiences

Middle of Funnel (MOF)
└─ Campaign: [Brand]_MOF_Engagement
   └─ Ad Set: IG/FB engagers + video viewers

Bottom of Funnel (BOF)
└─ Campaign: [Brand]_BOF_Conversion
   ├─ Ad Set: Retargeting — ATC, no purchase
   └─ Ad Set: Retargeting — visitors (30d)

Common mistakes to avoid

MistakeWhat it costs you
Running ads from a personal ad accountBan risk, and no business-level protection
Wrong timezone on the ad accountReporting drifts and budget burns at the wrong hours
Duplicate pixel eventsInflated conversions; the algorithm trains on bad signals
No CAPI set up20–40% of conversions lost post iOS 14
Skipping domain verificationRestricted targeting and lower ad trust
A single admin on the BMLock-out risk if that one person loses access
Set it up right once. Scale on top of it.
