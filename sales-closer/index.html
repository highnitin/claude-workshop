The role

Not cold calling. Closing.

We run paid workshops and coaching programs that teach performance marketers and business owners how to use AI to run lean, high-output agencies. Leads come in through ads, register for a webinar, attend — or don't — and then need a human to actually talk to them.

That human is you.

We are not looking for pressure tactics. We're looking for someone who listens carefully, asks better questions than the prospect is asking themselves, and closes without leaving anyone feeling sold to.

Responsibilities

You own the funnel, end to end

  1. Stage 01 · Registration → Show-up

    Before the webinar

    • Call and WhatsApp every new registrant within 2 hours of sign-up
    • Confirm attendance, understand their situation, note what they're stuck on
    • Run reminder sequences at every touchpoint — 24 hrs, 2 hrs, 10 minutes before
    • Push show-up rate. This is the biggest single lever on revenue, and it's yours
    • Flag high-intent leads to the trainer before the session starts
  2. Stage 02 · Live

    During the webinar

    • Work the chat — answer logistics, keep engagement up
    • Track who asks buying questions, who drops off, and at what minute
    • Drop the offer link and payment support at the right moments
  3. Stage 03 · Attendee → Enrollment

    After the webinar

    • Call every attendee and every no-show inside the follow-up window
    • Run structured discovery: where they are → what they've tried → what's blocking them → what changes if they fix it
    • Handle objections on price, time, trust, and "let me discuss with my family"
    • Walk fence-sitters through payment, EMI options, and deadline offers
    • Chase abandoned checkouts and failed payments the same day
    • Keep working warm leads for 7–14 days — most closes happen on the third conversation, not the first
  4. Stage 04 · Enrollment → Retention

    The community

    • Onboard every new enrollment into the WhatsApp community within 24 hours
    • Keep it alive — answer questions, surface wins, nudge quiet members, remind for live sessions
    • Spot members who've gone silent and re-engage them before they churn
    • Collect testimonials and result screenshots as they happen, not when we need them
    • Pass upsell and referral opportunities back to the team
  5. Stage 05 · Always

    Data and reporting

    • Log every conversation in the CRM. No exceptions, no "I'll update it later"
    • Keep lead stages accurate so the pipeline is always trustworthy
    • Share a daily number: dials, connects, conversations, closes, revenue
    • Report the objections you hear most, weekly — this changes our ad copy and webinar content directly
Who we're looking for

Requirements

Non-negotiable

  • Fluent, confident Hindi and Hinglish on call; English clean enough for a WhatsApp message
  • Willing to make 60–80 dials a day without being reminded
  • Comfortable being told no, repeatedly, and dialling the next number with the same energy
  • Basic comfort with WhatsApp Business, Google Sheets and a CRM — we'll train you on ours
  • Available evenings and weekends. That's when webinars run and when people pick up

Strongly preferred

  • Sold coaching, edtech, or any high-ticket program over the phone before
  • Some grasp of digital marketing — you won't run ads, but you should know what a lead, a pixel and a funnel are
  • A track record of hitting a number somewhere, in any field

What will make you fail here

  • Needing to be chased for updates
  • Treating follow-ups as optional
  • Talking more than you listen on a call
  • Promising things the program doesn't deliver, just to close
